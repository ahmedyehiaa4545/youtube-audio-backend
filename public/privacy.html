Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

At ReKaption, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our website, services, and integrations.

1. Information We Collect

  • OAuth Access Tokens: When you connect your TikTok account, we store secure authorization tokens required to interact with the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Basic Account Profile: We may access your TikTok display username and avatar for authentication and display purposes within the app.
  • Uploaded Media: Video files temporarily processed for transcription, clipping, and scheduled uploading.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to:

  • Process, render, and subtitle your requested videos.
  • Publish or draft videos to your TikTok account according to your scheduling timetable.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or video media to any third parties. All API interactions with TikTok are conducted over secure, encrypted HTTPS channels.

4. Revocation & Data Deletion

You can revoke ReKaption's permissions at any time via your TikTok application settings (Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions). Upon disconnection, your stored authorization tokens are permanently deleted.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, please email: privacy@rekaption.com
